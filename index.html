How I think

I'm deeply data-driven and naturally analytical.

I use data to help teams get clear on what actually matters. When I look at a business, I try to understand the key levers driving the most important metrics, and use the learnings to figure out what to do next.

I'm at my best helping teams pressure-test their assumptions, clarify trade-offs, and focus on the highest-leverage opportunities.

How I work

I move fluidly between strategy and execution.

I don't believe in tightly scoped, static projects. Startups move too quickly. Instead, I embed with teams in a flexible way, adapting my priorities as we learn.

That might mean zooming out to sharpen go-to-market strategy, or getting deep into the weeds on a specific channel, experiment, or decision. My goal is to be a true extension of the team as priorities evolve.
